Minho Song

Hello! I am Minho Song, a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematics at Yonsei University, working under the mentorship of Joonkyung Lee. I'm interested in algebraic combinatorics, with a specific focus on Riordan group theory, symmetric functions, representation theory, and combinatorial aspects of (generalized) orthogonal polynomials.

Papers → Curriculum vitae ↗

Research interests

Riordan groups

Group structures, matrix methods, total positivity, and combinatorial reciprocity.

Symmetric functions

Grothendieck polynomials, Schur positivity, representation theory, and refined bases.

Orthogonal polynomials

Moments, reciprocity, lattice-path models, and generalized families of type RII.

Combinatorial models

Dyck and Schröder paths, rational Catalan objects, Deograms, and bijections.
