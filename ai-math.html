Thematic Program on AI and Mathematics

I joined the Algebra, Combinatorics & Topology week (Aug 3–7) and worked with a group studying the dimension of the Fomin–Kirillov algebra using computational experiments and AI-assisted exploration.

I built the FK Edge-Heap Lab with AI for our group's work on the Fomin–Kirillov algebra. We used it to explore words and reductions toward the goal of proving that FK6 is infinite-dimensional.
